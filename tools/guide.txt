Voltworks Quarry

Digs a square pit behind it, layer by layer, down to bedrock.

The front faces you; the pit starts at the back.
---
Setting Up

Power it with Copper Cable: 20 J/t while digging (tier 2 power: two Generators, Thermal or a Reactor).

Put a chest on any side: the quarry fills it by itself. Item pipes work too.
---
How It Digs

9x9, from the quarry's height down. Harder blocks take longer.

Bedrock is skipped. Water and lava are cleared. Chests in the way are emptied into the output.

A redstone signal pauses it.
---
Upgrades

Range: +8 wide (up to 33x33).
Fortune: one level each, up to III, +50% power.
Silk Touch: blocks drop themselves, double power.

Speed, Capacity and Ejector upgrades work too.
---
Tips

The GUI shows the layer, size and blocks mined.

Turning the quarry or adding a Range upgrade restarts from the top; dug-out air is skipped fast.

Keep the area's chunks loaded.
